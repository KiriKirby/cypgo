The title records 1,200 bacterial sequences with 95 confidential entries removed and 27 archaeal entries included. The public document physically contains 1,152 explicit CYP-labelled blocks. They are retained in source order, including variants, renamed entries, pseudogenes, fragments and repeated names; the historical headline count is not used to discard source blocks. Organism labels come from each block header, with eleven explicitly reviewed exceptional headers (environmental samples, abbreviated Sorangium identifiers, and SP0881). Protein is accepted only from the longest uninterrupted uppercase residue run inside that exact block after removing Word wrapping, standalone residue numbering and phase markers. Annotation, similarity prose and accessions interrupt a run. Only terminal stop markers are removed; internal stops, X, gaps and question marks remain literal. No sequence is translated, repaired, deduplicated or fetched elsewhere.
